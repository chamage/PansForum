Community guidelines

The Agora works because people treat each other well. These few rules keep it that way.

1. Be kind and respectful

Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech — ever.

2. Help others learn, don't do their work

Explaining concepts and sharing study resources is great. Posting answers to ongoing exams, tests or graded assignments is not.

3. Protect privacy

Don't share anyone's personal information (phone numbers, addresses, private photos, grades) without their consent.

4. Keep it relevant

Post in the community that fits best. No spam, self-promotion floods or misleading titles.

5. Respect copyright

Don't upload paid textbooks, lecturers' slides you're not allowed to share, or other copyrighted material.

Reporting

See something that breaks these rules? Use the Report button. Moderators review every report. Repeated or serious violations lead to removal of content or accounts.
